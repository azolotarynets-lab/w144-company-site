Our way

Who we are and how we work.

An introduction for our partners and for the people of our community: who we are, how we work and what we are building.

.01

One family

A house built by one family.

W144 was founded by one family and is still run by it. Since 2022 it has grown into five projects, more than thirty people and more than 300,000 clients. It keeps growing on the values it began with: things done properly, and made for people.

  • Alexander ZolotarynetsFounder
  • Serhii ZolotarynetsCEO
  • Mikhail ZolotarynetsHead of Engineering

.02

Boutique

A boutique, not a corporation.

We do not want to become a conglomerate where the people at the top can no longer reach what happens below. Here they know everyone in the house and take part in every project. As we grow, we add small teams instead of making one team bigger, so that every contact with W144 still feels like a house of a few people.

.03

Authored products

Products we needed ourselves.

Every product of the house is authored. Each exists because we could not find what we needed on the market, and we are its first users. Each was built by a professional who knows that field from the inside, and that is where its quality comes from.

.04

Quality

Everyone has their own idea of good enough. Ours is set by quality and dignity.

Quality as the measure.

Making a product for ourselves is where we start, not the standard we hold it to. Everyone has their own idea of good enough; ours is set by quality and dignity. The Academy of market power works like a laboratory: we study each niche, the cases that are not obvious and the people who buy, and only then put out a product we can stand behind.

.05

One measure

The same measure everywhere.

The measure we hold our products to also holds for our partnerships, our deals and everything we say in the name of W144: honesty, and respect for the people on the other side. We speak about what we make as it is.

.06

Market power

Lasting market power.

We are after the right ways of working, the ones that give a business true and lasting market power: the software for its decisions, the agencies for its position and for what its product earns. We build the system first and grow it after.

For partners

You work directly with the people who run the house, and every deal is held to the same measure as our products.
